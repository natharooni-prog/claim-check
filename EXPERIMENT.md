# Claim Check one-week experiment (his rules, 2026-09-24)

Clock starts at deploy. After seven days, kill or keep.

## Kill if
- No independent agent ever gets past /quote, or
- Every settled /check is self traffic, or
- Buyers ask for raw funding history instead of a verdict.

## Keep if
- Even a few humans let an agent pay /check more than once.

## What counts (his words, 2026-09-24)
Do NOT count: my own verification traffic, Muse's tests, or anything I
intentionally send at it. No promotion, no demos, no shepherding anyone
through the flow. The experiment is whether strangers/independent agents
discover enough value to pay $0.25 on their own.

## Measurement
The revenue ledger records buyer address per settled check. Self addresses
(agent wallet 0x9f7e7200Adcf5c073981B902b8E8B471A458E528 and any test
wallets) are excluded from the organic count. Weekly ping reports organic
and self separately.
